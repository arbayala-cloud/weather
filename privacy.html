Privacy Policy

My Weather

My Weather is a personal weather app made by Ashley Ayala. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, sell, rent or share any personal information.

Saved on your device

Your saved places, nicknames and theme are saved in your browser on your own device. They never leave it unless you choose to sync or back them up.

Weather lookups

To show weather, the app sends the ZIP code, city name or map coordinates you look up to these free public weather services: Open-Meteo (forecast and city search), the U.S. National Weather Service at weather.gov (current conditions and alerts) and Zippopotam.us (ZIP code lookup). It sends no name, email or other personal details. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, the app uses it only to save a copy of your saved places and theme to a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive, so they match across your devices. The app requests the drive.appdata permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates in that hidden folder. It cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files. Your email address is shown in the app only so you know which account is syncing.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It is never sent to, or stored on, any server run by the app's maker. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can sign out of sync at any time from the app menu. To remove the app's saved data from Google Drive, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find My Weather and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing your browser's site data removes the copy on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
